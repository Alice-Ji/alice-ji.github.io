Portfolio


I take a product-first approach to behavioral research: start with real interfaces, test real tradeoffs, and let user behavior—not assumptions—drive design decisions.

My work spans three connected areas: (1) Choice Architecture and Decision Friction, (2) Social Influence in Monetized Platforms, and (3) User Autonomy in Interactive Systems using product-like prototypes to study how design shapes what people notice, choose, and buy. I build and instrument product-like prototypes in HTML/JS/React for behavioral telemetry (clicks, scrolling, dwell time, comments, eye-tracking, sentiment), and evaluate design decisions through user studies. My work shows how strong user experience can align with strong revenue outcomes.
